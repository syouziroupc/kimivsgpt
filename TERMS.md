# Terms of Use — Kimi vs GPT Answer Auditor

Last updated: 2026-09-08

Kimi vs GPT Answer Auditor is an advisory quality-control tool. It provides concise criticism of a proposed AI answer direction; it is not an authoritative source and does not replace independent verification.

## Intended use

The service is intended to help identify material weaknesses such as unsupported assumptions, anchoring, missing alternatives, stale facts, violated constraints, scope drift, or overconfidence before a final answer is produced.

The external critic is not intended to write the user's final answer, perform the user's research, generate a complete code implementation, or replace the primary model's work.

## No guarantee of correctness

A review may be incomplete, incorrect, or unnecessary. The calling model and user remain responsible for evaluating criticism against available evidence and for verifying consequential factual claims.

## High-stakes use

For legal, medical, financial, security-sensitive, safety-critical, or otherwise consequential decisions, the service should be treated only as an additional check and not as professional advice or a substitute for qualified review.

## Acceptable use

Do not intentionally send credentials, secrets, hidden chain-of-thought, unnecessary personal information, full private transcripts, or data you are not authorized to process.

## Availability

The service may be rate-limited, modified, interrupted, or unavailable. When the external review tool is unavailable, the accompanying skill may fall back to a limited internal counter-check and must not represent that fallback as an independent external review.
